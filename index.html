Featured Project

Nikunj

Student accommodation & relocation platform — concept

Status: Concept / In progress

An idea for a student-focused accommodation and relocation platform designed to help students and people moving to a new city discover more trustworthy accommodation and useful local services.

Problem

Students relocating to a new city often struggle to find reliable hostels, PGs, rooms, flats, rental properties, and food or tiffin options.

Solution

Nikunj aims to bring accommodation discovery and useful student-focused services into one platform — including hostel, PG, room, and flat listings with rent details, photos, reviews, location information, and verified listings.

Technologies explored / used during development

HTML CSS JavaScript Node.js Next.js Clerk APIs Vercel Render
